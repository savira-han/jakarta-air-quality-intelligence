Both sensors exhibited similar daily patterns throughout October, with relatively lower daily averages during the first half of the month, elevated values around October 15–22, a decline around October 24–26, and another increase on October 27–28 before ending the month at lower levels.

Lab. Lingkungan 3 generally recorded higher daily mean values than Lab. Lingkungan, with the difference particularly noticeable during periods of elevated observations.

Both sensors recorded their highest daily averages on October 17 and their lowest on October 31, indicating similar temporal patterns during the observation period.

Both sensors recorded elevated daily averages during October 15–22, with the highest values concentrated around October 15, 17, and 21.

Lab. Lingkungan 3 consistently recorded higher daily means during this period, reaching 87.30 on October 17, compared to 78.48 at Lab. Lingkungan.

### Declining Period: October 24–26

Daily averages declined considerably between October 24 and October 26 across both sensors.

Lab. Lingkungan recorded daily means between 41.89 and 50.55, while Lab. Lingkungan 3 recorded values between 44.75 and 53.35.

This was one of the lowest-average periods of the month.

### Late-Month Increase: October 27–28

Both sensors experienced an increase in daily averages on October 27–28.

Lab. Lingkungan recorded 65.53 on October 27 and 71.82 on October 28, while Lab. Lingkungan 3 recorded 74.49 and 85.65, respectively.

The increase was followed by a decline on October 29–31.

## 3. Daily Variability

Several days recorded relatively large daily ranges and standard deviations, indicating substantial variation in PM2.5 observations within individual days.

Notable examples include:

- October 4: Lab. Lingkungan 3 recorded a daily maximum of 143.0 and a daily range of 95.2.

- October 21: Lab. Lingkungan recorded a daily maximum of 131.0 and a daily range of 80.4.

These days represent periods of notable intraday variability and may warrant further investigation during elevated observation analysis.

## 4. Data Observation

Observation counts varied across days, particularly for Lab. Lingkungan 3 during the first week of October and toward the end of the month.

While most days contained 24 observations, some days had fewer recorded observations. This should be considered when interpreting daily statistics, as differences in observation counts may affect the representativeness of daily averages and variability.

## 5. Summary

- Both sensors exhibited broadly similar daily trends throughout October.
- PM2.5 daily averages increased during October 15–22, declined during October 24–26, and increased again on October 27–28.
- October 17 was the highest-average day for both sensors, while October 31 was the lowest-average day.

- Several days contained notable peaks and large daily ranges, particularly October 4, 7, 16, and 21, which may be relevant for further elevated observation analysis.

These findings describe temporal patterns in the recorded PM2.5 observations without establishing the causes of the observed changes.

# Hourly PM2.5 Trend Analysis — October 2026

## 1. Hourly Trend Overview

Both sensors exhibited a similar hourly pattern, with PM2.5 values generally increasing during the early morning, declining throughout the late morning and afternoon, and increasing again during the evening.

However, Lab. Lingkungan 3 consistently recorded higher hourly averages than Lab. Lingkungan, particularly during the early morning and nighttime periods.

## 2. Key Findings

### Highest and Lowest Hourly Averages

| Metric | Lab. Lingkungan | Lab. Lingkungan 3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Highest hourly mean | 72.66 (08:00) | 80.78 (02:00) |
| Lowest hourly mean | 49.43 (16:00) | 53.20 (16:00) |

Both sensors recorded their lowest hourly averages at 16:00. Lab. Lingkungan reached its highest hourly average at 08:00, while Lab. Lingkungan 3 peaked earlier, at 02:00.

### Early Morning Pattern

Lab. Lingkungan recorded increasing hourly averages from midnight through the morning, reaching approximately 70–73 between 06:00 and 09:00.

Lab. Lingkungan 3 recorded comparatively higher values during the early morning, with hourly averages reaching 80.78 at 02:00 and remaining elevated through 07:00.

### Afternoon Decline

Both sensors experienced a gradual decline in hourly averages from late morning through the afternoon.

Lab. Lingkungan recorded its lowest hourly averages between 14:00 and 17:00, while Lab. Lingkungan 3 recorded its lowest values between 14:00 and 16:00.

This represents the most consistent lower-value period across both sensors.

### Evening Increase

Hourly averages increased again from approximately 18:00 onward.

Lab. Lingkungan recorded a gradual increase from 52.33 at 18:00 to 58.86 at 21:00.

Lab. Lingkungan 3 recorded a more pronounced increase, reaching 68.03 at 21:00.

## 3. Hourly Variability

Hourly standard deviations indicate that variation was generally higher during the early morning and nighttime hours.

Lab. Lingkungan 3 exhibited particularly high variability between 01:00 and 07:00, with hourly standard deviations ranging approximately from 24 to 31.

In contrast, both sensors recorded lower variability during the afternoon, particularly around 14:00–17:00.

## 4. Data Observation

Observation counts varied across hours. Most hourly groups contained observations from approximately 30–31 days for Lab. Lingkungan and 24–31 days for Lab. Lingkungan 3.

The differences in observation counts should be considered when comparing hourly averages, particularly for hours with fewer available observations.

## 5. Summary

- Both sensors exhibited a similar hourly pattern, with higher values during the early morning and evening and lower values during the afternoon.
- Lab. Lingkungan peaked at 08:00, while Lab. Lingkungan 3 peaked at 02:00.
- Both sensors recorded their lowest hourly averages at 16:00.
- Lab. Lingkungan 3 generally recorded higher hourly averages and greater variability, particularly during the early morning and nighttime.
- The afternoon period, approximately 14:00–17:00, represented the lowest-value period for both sensors.

These findings describe the aggregated hourly patterns observed during October 2026. They do not establish the causes of these patterns or whether they consistently occurred on individual days.
